# Tian Tan Buddha — content scope revised 2026-08-31

## Current release

Transport-only suggestions subsequently restored: MTR to Tung Chung, NLB 23 from Tung Chung Tat Tung Road Bus Terminus, NLB 2 from Mui Wo Ferry Pier and NLB 21 from Tai O, all to Ngong Ping. Bus endpoints were checked against local open-data routes 1727/1725/1726; Tung Chung is station TUC in the bundled MTR open station catalogue. These are independently worded route suggestions, not restoration of the former website-derived visitor descriptions or ferry/cable-car guidance. No walking time, exit number or guaranteed connection is included.

Website-derived attraction descriptions, opening hours, step counts, phone details, walking estimates, exit guidance and ferry/cable-car connection instructions have been removed from the UI and unused localization entries at the user's request. The planner keeps Tung Chung as its preset, but now describes its MTR-only scope without reproducing onward visitor guidance.

The page retains custom cards, links to third-party official websites, and route navigation based on the existing open transport dataset. Links are not a claim of affiliation or permission to reproduce website content. Commercial reuse permission for visitor website material has NOT been established; factual verification is not licence verification. Do not restore that material without permission or an appropriate legal review. The Ngong Ping 360 expansion is paused pending the same review.

## Tsz Shan Monastery — first page slice checked 2026-09-01

- Official visit, registration, directions and guidelines page:
  https://www.tszshan.org/home/new/en/visit.php
- Official registration service:
  https://registration.tszshan.org/?locale=en_US
- The official directions page identifies GMB 20T as a direct weekday service to
  the monastery entrance, excluding public holidays. It identifies GMB 20B/20C,
  KMB 75K/275R and resident service NR532 as nearby services requiring an onward
  walk; 275R is listed for Sundays and public holidays. The app independently
  summarizes only these route classifications and sends visitors to the official
  page for current times and detailed directions.
- Opening hours, admission terms, registration instructions, accessibility details,
  walking times, fares and visitor guidelines are not reproduced. The page links
  to the official sources instead. No live-arrival claim is made.
- The in-app route browser uses exact route-number and operator guards: GMB 20T is
  direct/limited, GMB 20B/20C and KMB 75K/275R are nearby. It does not use fuzzy
  destination or proximity matching. NR532 remains an official-link-only item
  because it is not represented in the current in-app open-data route catalogue.
