- KMB official HK1/HK2 guide: <https://www.kmb.hk/storage/files/HK2%20Guide_compressed.pdf>
- Hong Kong Tourism Board night bus tour page: <https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/deals/hong-kong-night-bus-tour.html>

The first release identifies the dedicated sightseeing services and gives broad route coverage in independently worded summaries. Current fares, timetables, ticket conditions, detailed stops and vehicle availability remain on official external pages because they can change. Ordinary public-transport suggestions are deferred and will be presented separately from sightseeing products.

These entries open the app's existing route details and ETA where supported. They are labelled regular public transport, not tours; broad area coverage does not guarantee a particular view or attraction visit.
