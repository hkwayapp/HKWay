# Cross-Boundary Coach sources

Checked 2026-09-02.

## In-app short-haul catalogue

The five Huanggang corridors, their broad Hong Kong boarding locations and operators come from the Transport Department's current route pages:

- Mong Kok: <https://www.td.gov.hk/en/transport_in_hong_kong/land_based_cross_boundary_transport/access_to_lok_ma_chau_control_point/mong_kok_route/index.html>
- Yau Tsim: <https://www.td.gov.hk/en/transport_in_hong_kong/land_based_cross_boundary_transport/access_to_lok_ma_chau_control_point/yau_tsim_route/index.html>
- Kwun Tong: <https://www.td.gov.hk/en/transport_in_hong_kong/land_based_cross_boundary_transport/access_to_lok_ma_chau_control_point/kwun_tong_route/index.html>
- Wan Chai: <https://www.td.gov.hk/en/transport_in_hong_kong/land_based_cross_boundary_transport/access_to_lok_ma_chau_control_point/wan_chai_route/index.html>
- Tsuen Wan: <https://www.td.gov.hk/en/transport_in_hong_kong/land_based_cross_boundary_transport/access_to_lok_ma_chau_control_point/tsuen_wan_route/index.html>

Mutable stops, times, frequencies, fares and concessions remain on those official pages rather than being copied into the app.

## Long-haul and official directories

- Transport Department licensed coach operators by control point: <https://www.td.gov.hk/en/transport_in_hong_kong/land_based_cross_boundary_transport/enquiries/index.html>
- HZMB cross-boundary public transport: <https://www.hzmb.gov.hk/en/cross-boundary.html>
- Trans-Island Chinalink official FAQ/service information: <https://www.chinalink.hk/faq.php?lang=en>
- ONEBUS official Hong Kong–Macao service page: <https://www.onebus.hk/en/home>

No common documented public route, timetable, booking or live-arrival API was found. HK Way therefore provides an offline service catalogue and external official links only. It does not sell tickets, imply live ETA, reproduce commercial timetables, or guarantee that a listed trip is operating.
