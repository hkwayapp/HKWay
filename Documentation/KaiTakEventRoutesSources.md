# Kai Tak Event Routes sources

Checked 2026-09-02 (Hong Kong time).

- Kai Tak Sports Park official events page: <https://www.kaitaksportspark.com.hk/events-tickets>
- Kai Tak Sports Park official getting-here page: <https://www.kaitaksportspark.com.hk/getting-here>
- Transport Department special traffic news: <https://www.td.gov.hk/en/special_news/spnews.htm>
- Citybus official Kai Tak Sports Park route guide: <https://www.citybus.com.hk/app/ktsp-transportation/en>
- KMB official Kai Tak SP route notice for 28 February and 1 March 2026 (expired): <https://kmb.hk/storage/files/Kai%20Tak%20SP-routes%20Feb%202026.pdf>

No current event-specific official transport notice could be verified for inclusion. The first release therefore shows an explicit empty state and official links. It does not infer routes from previous events or claim live availability.

An event may be added only when an official event-specific notice confirms its date, venue, arrival or post-event phase, route/operator, and exact boarding or alighting area. Expired arrangements are filtered by their official end date.

The standing catalogue lists only route identities and general destinations found in those operator publications: KMB SP1, SP3, SP5A, SP6 and SP7; Citybus SP2, SP2A, SP5B, SP9 and SP11; and SP10/SP12 published by both operators for the checked event. All remain labelled event-day-only and unconfirmed. Dates, departure times, fares, stops and boarding points from expired notices are not reproduced as current information. Expired operator notices are retained here as research sources but are not linked from route cards in the app.
